What I fix

  • Theme health

    Move a store off a retired theme (Debut, Supply, Minimal, Pop) onto a current Online Store 2.0 theme without losing its look, or patch the old one where a rebuild is not needed.

  • Product pages that answer questions

    Shipping times, returns, sizing and specs next to the buy button, plus a gallery, reviews and a sticky add-to-cart on phones.

  • Speed

    Lazy-loaded and correctly sized images, old script libraries removed, and render-blocking code moved out of the way.

  • Search and sharing

    One H1 per page, clean titles and descriptions, product structured data and a share image so links look right in search results and messages.

  • Accessibility basics

    Descriptive alt text, a skip link, keyboard-usable menus and carts, and readable colour contrast.

  • Small fixes, done properly

    Broken characters, duplicate titles, missing policy pages, clumsy navigation. Changes are made on a copy of the theme and shown to you before anything goes live.

How it works

  1. Free audit

    I go through your public storefront and send a short list of specific problems, each with the page it is on and why it matters.

  2. You pick

    Choose the fixes you care about. One item or the whole list. Nothing starts until you say so.

  3. Preview, then publish

    I work on a duplicate of your theme. You get a preview link, check it on your own phone, and publish when you are happy.
